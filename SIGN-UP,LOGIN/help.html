Frequently asked questions

How do I screen my child?

Open Dashboard and start a screening for your child. Answer each question about what your child does today. Results appear under Results when the screening is complete.

How do I book or change an appointment?

Go to Appointments to book with a pediatrician, or to reschedule or cancel an existing booking. You will get a notification when the clinic confirms it.

How do I pay for a consultation?

After booking, choose Walk-in (pay at the clinic cashier) or E-wallet on the payment page. E-wallet payments are checked by the clinic before they show as paid.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password?. We will email you a 6-digit code that lets you set a new password. The code expires after 15 minutes.

How do I change my password while signed in?

Open your Profile and use Change Password.

How do I let a family member see my child's records?

Parents can open Settings › Family to add a family member, or Guardian Management to invite someone who already has an account.
