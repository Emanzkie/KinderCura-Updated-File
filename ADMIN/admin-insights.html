What this page is, and what it is not

  • These figures describe rule-based screening scores. Each score is fixed arithmetic over the answers a parent gave, placed into a band by a fixed cutoff. Nothing here is predicted, estimated, or learned from data.
  • The band cutoffs are pending confirmation from the consultant pediatrician and are recorded as unconfirmed in constants/scoring.js. They should not be described as clinically validated.
  • Section 4 reports agreement between two recorded judgements — the screening band and the reviewing pediatrician's conclusion. It is not a validation against a diagnostic reference standard, and it is not an accuracy figure.
  • All counts are aggregate. No child or parent is identified anywhere on this page.
  • There is no geographic breakdown. Neither a child record nor a user account carries a residential location, and nothing is substituted in its place.

Filters are held in the page address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared and survives a drill-down. The date range defaults to the last 12 months. Sex and age band describe children — they do not apply to Section 1, because a user account carries neither field. Each section states which filters affected it.
