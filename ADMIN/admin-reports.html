Reading these numbers correctly Each KPI above counts a different kind of record — a user account, a child/patient record, an assessment session, or an appointment booking — not a set of the same children. One child can have zero, one, or many assessments and appointments, so these totals cannot be added or subtracted to infer facts like "how many children were never assessed." To see a specific child's own assessment count, use Centralized Patient Reports below, which reports per-child. Units: accounts = unique user logins · child records = unique children · assessments = screening sessions · appointments = booking records · percentages = share of the total stated beside them · amounts = money recorded on payment records, in their stored currency (Clinic Reports only). Periods: Clinic Reports follow their own period picker, Patient Reports follow their filters, and the late-development and diagnosis reports use the screenings date range printed in their notes; everything else is all-time.

Clinic Reports

Registered clinic records, the pediatricians linked to each clinic, and payment records grouped by clinic.

Registered Clinics

This report shows the distinct clinics recorded in the database and the pediatricians linked to each. A clinic is one record an admin registered, counted once by its ID no matter how many pediatricians are linked to it, so the clinic count is not a count of pediatrician accounts. The shared clinic name in Settings (printed on receipts) and the clinic names pediatricians typed at sign-up are not clinic records and are never counted.

Reporting period: all dates.
